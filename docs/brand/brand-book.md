# LevelUp — Brand Book

## 1. Brand Essence

**Name:** LevelUp
**Tagline:** "Your life, gamified."
**Mission:** Transform everyday habit-building from a chore into an adventure. Every completed task is XP earned. Every broken streak is a boss defeated. Growth should feel like play.

**Core Values:**
- **Progress over perfection** — Missed a day? You have freeze tokens. Rejected proof? Try again. The system rewards consistency, not perfection.
- **Transparency** — AI verification is honest. XP calculations are visible. No hidden mechanics.
- **Social growth** — Competition with friends accelerates personal development. We level up together.
- **Adaptive** — Your circumstances change. The system adapts to your energy, health, workload, and life stress — not the other way around.

## 2. Target Personas

### Primary: "The Ambitious Starter" — Age 18-28
- **Name:** Alex
- **Profile:** University student or early-career professional. Wants to build better habits (gym, reading, coding practice) but quits after 2 weeks every time.
- **Pain point:** Traditional habit apps feel boring. No feedback loop. No social accountability.
- **Motivation:** Visual progress, leaderboards with friends, leveling up feels rewarding.
- **Device:** Mobile-first (80%), desktop for deep work sessions.
- **UX need:** Quick task completion (<5 seconds), satisfying animations on completion, social comparison.

### Secondary: "The Structured Achiever" — Age 28-45
- **Name:** Sarah
- **Profile:** Working professional, possibly a parent. Juggles career, fitness, family, and personal projects. Uses systems and frameworks.
- **Pain point:** Existing tools don't account for bad days. Life is unpredictable, but habit apps expect robot-level consistency.
- **Motivation:** The circumstances system that adjusts expectations. Freeze tokens for rough days. Analytics showing long-term trends.
- **Device:** Desktop during work, mobile evenings/weekends.
- **UX need:** Clear data visualization, minimal friction, respects her time.

### Tertiary: "The Gamer Who Wants Real Progress" — Age 16-30
- **Name:** Jordan
- **Profile:** Loves games, RPGs, and progression systems. Would love to see real-life progress visualized the same way.
- **Pain point:** Gaming skills don't transfer to real life. Wants that dopamine hit from real achievements.
- **Motivation:** XP system, levels, achievements, the RPG aesthetic done tastefully.
- **Device:** Mobile-first, Discord-generation UI expectations.
- **UX need:** Game-like feedback, satisfying animations, collectible achievements.

## 3. Brand Personality

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Energetic** | Not childish. Vibrant without being loud. Think Spotify Wrapped meets Duolingo. |
| **Honest** | The AI tells you "this isn't convincing proof" gently but directly. No sugarcoating. |
| **Supportive** | Encouraging messages that aren't cringe. "Streak at risk" not "You're failing!" |
| **Smart** | The system learns your patterns. It knows when you're pushing too hard. |
| **Premium** | Feels high-quality. Every pixel is intentional. No default-looking anything. |

## 4. Voice & Tone

**In-app messages:**
- ✅ "Task verified! +25 XP earned"
- ✅ "Your streak is on the line — complete today's task to keep it alive"
- ✅ "Looks like you've had a heavy week. XP requirements adjusted by 15%."
- ❌ "Congratulations!! You're AMAZING! Keep going! 🎉🎉🎉" (too much)
- ❌ "Error: task not found" (too robotic)

**Error states:**
- ✅ "We couldn't verify that proof. Try uploading a clearer photo."
- ✅ "Something went wrong on our end. We're on it."
- ❌ "An unexpected error occurred" (vague)

**Social:**
- ✅ "Jordan completed 5 tasks today. Can you beat that?"
- ❌ "Your friend is better than you" (negative framing)

## 5. Naming Conventions

| Element | Name | Reasoning |
|---------|------|-----------|
| XP | XP (Experience Points) | Universal gaming term, immediately understood |
| Levels | Levels | Standard RPG progression |
| Streaks | Streaks | Familiar from Snapchat, Duolingo |
| Freeze Token | Shield | "Shield your streak" — more visual, game-like |
| Daily Challenge | Quest | RPG language, feels like an adventure |
| Proof | Proof | Serious word, implies accountability |
| Circumstances | Context | "Your life context" — less clinical than "circumstances" |

## 6. Logo Direction

**Concept:** An upward arrow merged with a level-up indicator. Clean, geometric, works at 16px favicon and 200px hero.
**Style:** Flat with subtle gradient. No 3D, no shadows on the logo itself.
**Icon-only:** Arrow upward with a small star/spark at the tip.
**Wordmark:** "LevelUp" in Space Grotesk (the display font), with "Level" in regular weight and "Up" in bold.

## 7. Illustration Style

- **Geometric characters** — Simple, diverse, no faces. Think Notion or Linear illustrations.
- **Limited palette** — Uses only brand colors + neutrals.
- **Motion-ready** — Shapes that can animate (circles that pulse, arrows that rise).
- **Context-aware** — Empty states show relevant illustrations (no tasks = relaxed character, many tasks = busy character).
